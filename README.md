## 📋 Task Overview & Objectives

According to the **CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship Requirements**, Task 2 requires building a comprehensive educational module covering:

1. **Online Training Module**: An intuitive, engaging presentation platform focused on phishing awareness.
2. **Email & Website Attack Detection**: Techniques to identify fraudulent email senders, suspicious subject lines, fake domains, typosquatting, and deceptive URLs.
3. **Social Engineering Tactics**: Deep breakdown of psychological triggers (urgency, fear, authority, baiting, pretexting, quid pro quo).
4. **Best Practices & Defense**: Actionable enterprise and individual defense strategies (MFA, password managers, verification protocols).
5. **Real-World Case Studies & Interactive Quiz**: Real enterprise breaches analyzed with interactive quizzes and instant remediation explanations.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🎓 Interactive 5-Part Training Course
- **Module 1 — Anatomy of Phishing**: Fundamental mechanics, threat vectors, delivery methods, and why phishing remains the #1 initial breach vector.
- **Module 2 — Spotting Phishing Emails**: Interactive visual breakdown of spoofed sender headers, display-name spoofing, urgent calls to action, mismatched hyperlinks, and dangerous attachments.
- **Module 3 — Detecting Fake Websites**: Address bar inspection, punycode attacks, homograph attacks, typosquatting, SSL/TLS certificate myths, and fake login forms.
- **Module 4 — Social Engineering Tactics**: Exploration of psychological manipulation techniques including Pretexting, Spear Phishing, Whaling, Baiting, Vishing, Smishing, and Quid Pro Quo.
- **Module 5 — Best Practices & Response**: Step-by-step incident response playbook, password hygiene, multi-factor authentication (MFA), email signing (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), and reporting channels.

### 2. 🔍 Real-World Attack Case Studies
Interactive breakdown of landmark cybersecurity incidents:
- **Google & Facebook ($100M+ Vendor Invoice Scam)** — Business Email Compromise (BEC) and forged corporate invoices.
- **FACC CEO Fraud ($54M Breach)** — Executive impersonation and urgent fraudulent wire transfers.
- **Twilio SMS Phishing (Okta Breach)** — Smishing attack compromising employee credentials and bypass of basic 2FA.

### 3. 🎯 Interactive Knowledge Check Quiz
- Multi-question interactive scenario quiz testing real identification skills.
- Dynamic scoring with immediate feedback, rationale, and indicators explained for every answer choice.
- Personalized **Certificate of Completion** generated dynamically with participant name, score, and completion timestamp upon achieving a passing score.

### 4. 🎨 Modern Cyber Aesthetic & UX
- Glassmorphism dark mode with neon accents (cyan/purple glow).
- Smooth transitions, animated progress trackers, and responsive mobile-friendly layout.
- Custom-designed cybersecurity shield favicon and branded UI assets.

## 🛡️ Core Educational Content Summary

### Common Phishing Red Flags
| Indicator | What Attackers Do | How to Verify |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sender Email** | Display name spoofing (e.g. `CEO <hr-update@mail-xyz.com>`) | Check full email address and SPF/DKIM validation |
| **Hyperlinks** | Anchor text shows `paypal.com` but links to `paypa1-verify.net` | Hover before clicking; inspect actual destination URL |
| **Urgency / Threat** | *"Account suspended within 2 hours unless verified"* | Legitimate organizations do not demand instant action via email |
| **Attachments** | Obfuscated archives (`.zip`, `.iso`), macros in `.docm`, `.html` | Never enable macros; scan with sandbox/antivirus |
| **Domain Typos** | `micosoft.com`, `amaz0n.support`, `bank-security-portal.co` | Inspect root domain name carefully |
